Unpublished Work

Household Level Effects of Flooding: Evidence from Thailand

with Zhuldyz Ashikbayeva, Marei Fürstenberg, Albert Pierres and Stephan Thies

TVSEP Working Paper WP-022, 2020

Summary

Using household panel data from rural Northeast Thailand (2007–2016) combined with satellite-based flood measures, we estimate the causal effect of flooding on household income and expenditure. Floods reduce household income and raise spending on food and health. Households appear to adapt: it is deviations from usual flood levels, not flooding as such, that cause harm. Education beyond primary school offsets the negative effects.

Trade Models in the European Union

with Claudius Gräbner-Radkowitsch, Dennis Tamesberger, Philipp Heimberger and Jakob Kapeller

Economic Annals, 2022, 67(235): 7–36

Summary

Studying the factors behind differences in trade performance across 22 EU economies, the paper derives six "trade models" (primary goods, finance, flexible labour market, periphery, industrial workbench and high-tech) and traces their developmental and distributional dynamics. Wage shares shrink and inequality rises in most models; the high-tech model proves comparatively stable.
